Bree privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Bree is designed to work without an account or internet connection.

Information stored on your device

Bree may store completed exercise check-ins, optional saved reflections, and app preferences locally on your device. This information is used only to show your activity and remember your settings. Reflections are not saved unless you turn on “Save this reflection on this device.”

Bree does not include an analytics SDK, advertising SDK, account system, cloud database, or network service. Its rain, ocean, brown-noise, and forest soundscapes are generated locally in memory. Optional reminders use Apple’s local notification system and do not contact a server. Bree does not transmit check-ins or reflections to the developer or to third parties.

Local application information may be included in device backups according to your Apple device and backup settings.

Deletion

You can delete completed check-ins from Settings → Delete My Data. You can also remove the application to delete its local application container, subject to Apple device-backup behavior.

Health and emergency limitations

Bree provides general relaxation and wellbeing exercises. It does not diagnose or treat a medical condition and is not a substitute for professional medical care. Bree does not provide emergency or crisis response services.

Changes

If future versions add accounts, synchronization, analytics, subscriptions, or other data processing, this policy will be updated before those features are released.

Contact

Contact Bree support on GitHub.
