Bree terms

Terms and wellbeing boundaries

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Bree is a personal wellbeing companion. By using Bree, you understand that its exercises, soundscapes, reflections, and optional prayer are general informational tools for relaxation—not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency support.

Use thoughtfully

Choose only practices that feel comfortable. Stop any practice at any time. Do not use Bree while driving or when attention must stay on a safety-critical task.

Emergency support

Bree cannot monitor you, contact help, or prevent a panic attack. If you may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or an appropriate crisis service in your area.

Local content and availability

Bree’s core exercises and soundscapes are bundled or generated on-device. We may change or improve content in future versions, but we do not promise a particular result or outcome.
